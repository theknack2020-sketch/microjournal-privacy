Frequently Asked Questions

How do I write my daily entry?

Open the app and tap "Write Today's Sentence" on the Today tab. Type your sentence (up to 280 characters), optionally select a mood, and tap Save.

Can I edit today's entry?

Yes! Tap the pencil icon on your completed entry card to edit it. You can also change the mood.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts consecutive days where you've written an entry. Write every day to keep your streak alive! Missing a day resets the streak to zero.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Go to Settings → Micro Journal Pro → Restore Purchases. This will restore your subscription if you've previously purchased Pro.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Micro Journal to manage or cancel.

Is my journal data backed up?

Your entries are stored locally on your device. With Pro, you can export your journal as text using Settings → Export Journal. We recommend regular exports for backup.

How do I delete all my entries?

Go to Settings → Delete All Entries. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
